# Speisely Gastro-Bridge: Free Local Event Venue Outreach Playbook
*Targeting Event Locations, Castles, Barns & Industrial Venues in NRW & Berlin for Free "Preferred Caterer" Backlinks.*

---

## 1. Strategy & The Value Exchange
Event venues constantly get asked by couples and corporate event planners: *"Can you recommend a great caterer?"*
Most venues maintain a **"Partner & Caterer"** page on their website. 

**Why they link to Speisely & Partyservice Küpper for free:**
1. We give their clients an **instant digital event calculator** (saving the venue coordinator hours of back-and-forth).
2. We feature the venue in **Speisely Magazin** (`/magazin`) under *"Top Event Locations in NRW/Berlin"* (free publicity for the venue).
3. Verified culinary standards (100% Halal, BBQ live grilling, Vegan certified).
